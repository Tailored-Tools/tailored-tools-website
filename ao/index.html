The way people find businesses has changed

Finding a local service used to mean Google, a few websites, maybe a couple of phone calls. Increasingly, that's not how it works.

Before

The human does the searching

  • Types "electrician near me" into Google
  • Clicks through four or five websites
  • Reads each one, checks reviews, compares prices
  • Calls a couple to check availability
  • Picks one and books
Now

The AI does the searching

  • Person asks their AI assistant: "find me a reliable electrician available this week, who charges a fair call-out fee"
  • AI scans for businesses that match those criteria
  • Filters by location, availability, pricing signals, reviews
  • Presents 2 or 3 options to the person
  • Person picks one, often without visiting a website at all

This isn't about ranking. It's about inclusion. If the AI can't read the information about your business, you're not in the conversation at all.

And when AI does mention your business, is it accurate? Wrong prices, outdated services, confused with a competitor, that costs you customers too. AO works both sides: getting you recommended, and making sure the recommendation is right.

    What's the difference between AO and SEO?

    SEO helps you rank on Google. AO helps you get recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. They share some foundations (good content, consistent listings), but AI agents need specific signals that Google doesn't require in the same way. Think of it as: SEO puts you on the list. AO gets you recommended.

    Do you change my website design?

    No. Everything we add is invisible to your visitors. Your website looks exactly the same. We add a layer of data behind the scenes that AI agents can read directly. Your customers see nothing different. Agents see everything they need.

    How long does it take?

    The free score takes 2 working days. The full AO Audit takes 5 working days and includes a 20-minute call to walk through your results. Implementation time depends on complexity, but most local businesses are done within a week.

    Is this a one-time thing or ongoing?

    The audit and implementation are one-off. Once your data is in place, it stays. But AI agents update their knowledge constantly, so we offer monthly monitoring (£97/month) to track what they say about you, catch inaccuracies, and flag regressions. It's optional but recommended.

    I'm already on Google Maps. Isn't that enough?

    Google Business Profile helps, and it's one of several sources AI agents check. But AI also reads your website, review sites, directories, and social media. If those sources disagree, or your website has no AI-readable data, the agent can't confidently recommend you. GBP is a piece of the puzzle, not the whole picture.

    Will this guarantee AI recommends me?

    No. We won't promise that because it would be dishonest. What we guarantee is that your business will have the data that makes recommendations possible. Without it, you're excluded. With it, you're a candidate. The rest depends on your services, reviews, and competition.

    How do I know if AI is even mentioning my business?

    Ask it. Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini and type "Tell me about [your business name] in [your town]." Compare what it says to reality. Most business owners have never done this and are surprised by what they find. The biggest referrer to your business might never show up in your analytics.
