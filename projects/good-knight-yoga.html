Yoga, Breathwork & Meditation · Kent

Twelve answers on a Sunday. A live site within a day.

Jasmine Knight teaches yoga for the people who look after everyone else. She sent her brief on a Sunday evening, and had a working website with her name on it to react to within a day.

Visit goodknightyoga.co.uk →

The ask, and what was underneath it

What she asked for

A website for her classes, 1:1s, corporate sessions and retreats.

What was really going on
  • Her people are caregivers, mums and teachers. The site had to feel like her classes: calm, warm and a little playful.
  • Lots of ways in, each with different times, places and prices, plus a second teacher with classes of her own.
  • Timetables and retreat details change often. Waiting on someone else for every update would kill the momentum.

She needed a site that felt like stepping onto her mat, and that she could keep up to date herself.

goodknightyoga.co.uk

Her logo, redrawn as a constellation in a night sky. Knight, goodnight: the name was the brief.

What I built

A soft, nocturnal website built from her own story and words, with the practical parts quietly doing their job underneath.

A brand drawn from her name

Night sky, moon and candlelight, with a real named constellation for each of the people she teaches: the Crown, the Great Bear, the North Star, the Cup.

Every way in, clearly laid out

Classes, 1:1s and breathwork, corporate sessions and retreats, each with its own page, times and prices.

Two teachers, one mailing list

Jasmine's classes and Hazel's sit side by side, with enquiries routed through one place so contact details stay private.

She edits it herself

A simple admin page for retreats and class details. Jasmine updated a retreat's venue and deposit herself, no developer needed.

Forms that go straight to her

Enquiries, a 1:1 intake form and the mailing list all land in her own inbox.

Her domain, her account

She registered goodknightyoga.co.uk herself, with her own card, following a step-by-step walkthrough written just for her.

How it went

Sunday evening

Twelve answers: her services, her story, "calm, warm and playful", and a couple of websites she loves.

Within a day

A live site with her name on it: her story near-verbatim, her logo as a constellation, working forms, and clearly marked spaces for everything still to come.

The next morning

Her first notes arrive through the site's own feedback button. All of it is live by that evening.

7 August

goodknightyoga.co.uk launches, with privacy and terms pages, a class interest list and her intake form.
