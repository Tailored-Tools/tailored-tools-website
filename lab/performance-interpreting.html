A voice in the wings

Meet PIPA. Say it “PEE‑pa”.

PIPA is the Performance Interpreting Personal Assistant, an AI assistant I built for PI. It carries a name the way a person does, not like a brand stamp. It works in two places, for two kinds of people.

PIPA helps people. It never replaces interpreters.

For customers · on the website since May 2026

Ask, and it goes and looks.

A chat assistant grounded in PI’s own site content, with live tools of its own.

  • Searches upcoming interpreted events.
  • Looks up a venue’s access details.
  • Finds PI’s own signed explainer videos and plays them right in the chat. Only from PI’s own library, never a random link.
  • Builds a ready-made interpreter-request link.
  • When it can’t answer, it hands over to a person with a tidy summary of what was asked and what it already tried, and can pre-fill the contact form with the right enquiry type.
  • Rate-limited, and hardened against misuse.

A demo conversation with PIPA. Someone asks about interpreted comedy coming up, and PIPA searches upcoming interpreted events. They ask about step-free access, and PIPA looks up the venue's access details. They ask how booking works, and PIPA plays one of PI's own signed explainer videos in the chat. They say their event has no interpreter, and PIPA builds a ready-made request link. Finally they ask something PIPA can't answer, and PIPA hands over to a person with a summary and a pre-filled contact form.

For PI’s interpreters · since 27 August 2026

Help with the prep, not the performance.

Inside PI’s setlist and lyrics tool, “Ask PIPA” sits on every page, grounded in the tool’s own guide. Every question it can’t answer is logged, so the gaps become a to-do list.

For an interpreter preparing a song, it writes Song insights: themes, register, figurative language, cultural references, ambiguous lines with possible readings, and quality flags on the lyrics.

  • Works only from the stored lyrics. No outside claims.
  • Clearly labelled as AI-generated and unverified.
  • Cached, so it only regenerates when the lyrics change.
  • Interpreters keep their own private prep notes beside it.

A demo of a Song insights panel filling in, section by section: themes, register, figurative language, cultural references, ambiguous lines, and quality flags. It is labelled AI-generated and unverified. Beside it is a private prep notes card.

After the show

The crew that works through the night.

When the team goes home, the systems keep going. Here’s the night shift, sped up so a whole day goes round in about half a minute.

A 24-hour clock running fast. Small marks light every 30 minutes as app alerts are checked, bigger marks every two hours for image enrichment and the website listing refresh, and a gold marker at 2am for the nightly backup.
