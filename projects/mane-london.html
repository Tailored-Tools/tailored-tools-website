Before any code

A price list was the ask. I started with the salon.

Every touch on her new site comes from something I noticed about Holly and the way her studio runs. I kept her voice, too: the lines running across the ribbon above are hers.

I noticed

One price list is really three.

The same colour costs something different with a stylist, a senior stylist or a specialist.

So I made

A menu where you choose a stylist level and every colour price changes with the level you choose, all one tap away.

She runs the salon on WhatsApp.

I noticed

Printed prices go stale.

A hundred menus are only right until the next price change.

So I made

A "scan me" QR on the A5 menu. It opens straight onto her hair prices with a "Welcome from our printed menu" note, so every printed menu leads to today's prices.

I noticed

Her website was down.

It sat on a server she didn't control. Every change had meant waiting on someone else, and paying for each one.

So I made

Her domain hers. We moved it under her control and switched it over live, during the build. Now she sends changes on WhatsApp and they're live the same day. Hosting is included, and I keep an eye on it.
