Systemic Therapy & Educational Consultancy · Kent

A practice that books itself, without giving anything away

Velma Bennett is a systemic practitioner and education consultant. She needed a website people would trust with something personal, and a practice that ran itself between sessions.

Visit velmabennett.co.uk →

The ask, and what was underneath it

What she asked for

A professional website for her therapy and education work.

What was really going on
  • Two very different audiences under one name: families looking for therapy, and parents and schools looking for education advice.
  • Every booking meant back-and-forth: finding a time, taking payment, sending forms, sending a video link.
  • Intake and consent paperwork had to be right, and had to reach clients before the first session.
  • She would never publish her address, and most booking systems show it by default.

She didn't just need a website. She needed the admin around every session to happen without her.

velmabennett.co.uk

The live site. Every word of copy approved by Velma before launch.

What I built

A calm, careful website, and behind it a practice set up on Cliniko so the admin runs on its own. Everything is in Velma's name: domain, email, booking system and payments.

Two doors, one practice

Therapy and education consulting each get their own path, in their own language, connected by the idea that runs through all her work.

Book and pay in one go

Three pathways: a free 30-minute consultation, therapy, and education consulting. Each opens straight onto the right booking page, with payment taken at booking.

Paperwork that sends itself

Intake forms written with her and approved line by line, sent automatically with the booking confirmation, then reminders with the secure video link.

Private by design

Her address never appears: not on the site, not on the booking page, not in a single email. Sessions run by secure video, in person by arrangement.

Emails that look like her

Confirmations, reminders and cancellations branded to match the site, built to display properly even in fussy email apps.

Her own professional email

A hello@ address on her own domain, set up and handed over in person, so enquiries never land in a personal inbox.

How it went

Discovery

A live prototype with her name on it, so she could react to something real. Velma shaped every word before launch.

velmabennett.co.uk launches, after a face-to-face session to set up her email, booking system and payments together.

After her supervision

29 September

Online booking with payment goes live. Clients pick a pathway, choose a time, accept her terms and pay, all in one go.
