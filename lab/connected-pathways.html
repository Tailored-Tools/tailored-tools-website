Privacy by design

What I chose not to keep.

Her work depends on trust. So I built the systems around her to know as little as possible, starting with me.

  • Nowhere. Not on the site, the booking page or any email.

  • Switched off. Enquiries go only to her inbox.

  • Not kept. I hold no copy once she's set her own.

  • Never there. Her diary feeds her calendar as busy, and nothing else.

  • Not shown, on AFSP's own advice.

The roots

The parts you don't see.

Underneath the calm front, these hold everything up. They join up as you scroll, like roots.

Two intake forms

One for therapy, one for education. Written with her, approved line by line, built into Cliniko and sent automatically with the booking confirmation.

Secure video, built in

Confirmations and reminders carry a secure video link from Cliniko's own telehealth, which replaced Zoom.

Emails in her colours

Confirmation, reminder and cancellation emails in her navy, sage and cream. They're generated from a script, so they can be rebuilt exactly.

Terms, agreed as they book

Clients accept her 48-hour cancellation terms before the booking goes through.

Her own professional email

An address on her own domain, set up and handed over in person.

Everything in her name

The domain, the mailbox, Cliniko and Stripe. Everything that holds her data or her money belongs to her.
