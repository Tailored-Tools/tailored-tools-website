The wider PI estate

The Events App sits alongside the agency's other live properties, each one supporting a different part of how PI runs:

  • Main website, performanceinterpreting.co.uk, the agency's primary client-facing site for booking enquiries, services, and case studies.
  • PI Academy, academy.performanceinterpreting.co.uk, CPD and training platform for working BSL interpreters.
  • PI Events App, native iOS and Android (see the breakdown above).

About the agency

Performance Interpreting was founded by Marie Edwards. It is a BSL interpretation agency providing communication access for the Deaf community, at festivals, football matches, theatre, live music, conferences, and workplaces, across the UK and Ireland.

  • A network of 379 interpreters (346 BSL + 33 Irish)
  • Coverage across the UK and Ireland
  • Working with festivals, football clubs, live music tours, theatre, and corporate clients
In development

The platform underneath: PI OS

The Events App sits on top of a booking platform we are building for the agency, what we call PI OS. The interpreter database, the booking lifecycle, the staff portal, and the integrations live there. Parts of it are running in production today. Other parts are still being built.

We will write about PI OS properly when it is finished. For now, the app is the thing that is in customers' hands and earning its keep.
