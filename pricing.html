Questions people ask

If yours isn't here, ask. You'll get a straight answer.

Why "from"?

Because no two businesses need the same thing. The tier is the starting point. Your quote adds only what you need, each item with its own line, so you can see exactly what you're paying for and drop anything you don't want.

Do I have to pay before I see anything?

No. You answer a short brief, and within days you get a live website with your name on it. If it doesn't feel right, walk away. No invoice. How that works.

What if I only need something small?

Say so. Sometimes the honest answer is a smaller job, and I'll tell you. Sometimes the small ask turns out to be the visible end of a bigger problem: Mane London came to me for printed price lists. Either way, you decide with the price in front of you.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain, your accounts and your payments are registered to you and paid by you. If we ever part ways, you lose nothing.

Can I spread the setup cost?

Ask. Splitting the setup into a few payments is often possible, agreed in writing before the build starts.

What happens if I stop the care plan?

Everything stays yours. I'll help you move the site wherever you want it, with a plain-English handover so you or anyone else can look after it.

Want to see yours?

Twelve questions, ten minutes, nothing to pay. You'll see a live version before we talk money.
