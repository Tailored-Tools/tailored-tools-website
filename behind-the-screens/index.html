Questions to ask anyone quoting, including us.

Whoever builds it, ask these before you pay. Get the answers in writing.

Before you sign12 questions
  1. Is the price fixed? What isn't included?
  2. What will it cost each month to run, including hosting, support and updates?
  3. If it breaks on a Saturday, who fixes it, and how fast?
  4. Where are card details kept? They should sit with the payment provider, not on the website's own server.
  5. Who owns the code and the data, and can I take them with me?
  6. Is everything backed up every night, and has anyone practised restoring it?
  7. How do my clients, notes, future bookings and deposits move over from my old system?
  8. Will it hold health or other sensitive notes, and who can see them?
  9. Can I talk to a business like mine that you've built this for?
  10. Am I buying it or renting it? What happens if I stop paying?
  11. Who pays for each text and WhatsApp message, and roughly how much a month?
  12. What's the total over three years: setup plus every monthly cost?
