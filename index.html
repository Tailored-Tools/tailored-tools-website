About Tailored Tools

Tailored Tools is a Kent-based consultancy that helps small service businesses get the most out of technology — without the jargon, the overwhelm, or the eye-watering agency fees.

We work with sole traders, small teams, and service businesses who know they should be doing more with tech but don't have the time or inclination to figure it out themselves. That's where we come in.

Our background is in accessibility technology and service businesses, which means we understand both the tech side and the reality of running a small operation. We build practical solutions that actually get used, not shiny things that gather dust.
