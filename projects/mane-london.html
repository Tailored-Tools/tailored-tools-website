Hair & Beauty Studio · Bexley, London

Mane London didn't come to me for a website

Holly came in for a hundred printed price lists. The next day her salon had a new website, with the price list built in and a way to change it the same day.

Visit manelondon.co.uk →

The ask, and what was underneath it

What she asked for

A hundred A5 price lists at her new prices, designed and printed.

What was really going on
  • Her website was down, on a server she didn't control.
  • Every change meant waiting on someone else, and paying per change.
  • Prices depend on which stylist level you book, so one flat list could never tell a client what they'd actually pay.
  • Offers, team and prices change all the time. A printed list starts going out of date the day it arrives.

The price list wasn't the problem. The problem was that the salon changes constantly, and nothing it had could keep up.

manelondon.co.uk

The live site, built on Mane London's own gold-foil brand and the team's real photos.

What I built

A website shaped around how a salon actually takes bookings. Booking itself stays on Vagaro, which already worked, linked from every page.

Prices that suit your stylist

Pick a stylist and the whole menu shows that level's prices: hair, treatments and extensions. No more "from" prices that mean nothing.

Build a visit, send it by WhatsApp

Tap + on the services you want and the site adds them up, then sends the whole visit to the studio as a WhatsApp message.

A "This month" board

Offers, new treatments and last-minute gaps, sized for the artwork Holly already makes for Instagram. She sends it on WhatsApp and it's live the same day.

Subscriptions, explained plainly

Monthly plans with the rules written in plain English: who can join, moving appointments, stopping early. Fewer awkward conversations at the desk.

Paper and website from one price file

The printed price list she originally asked for and the website's menu come from the same source, so they can never disagree.

The small things clients ask

Parking codes you tap to copy into RingGo, live open or closed status, payment and access details, and the salon's 5-star Google reviews.

How it went

Wednesday

Holly asks about price lists, and what a website would cost, because changing her old one had become a nightmare.

Wednesday, same day

A live demo of her new site, with her real photos, logo and prices, is on her phone. The price is agreed in writing before any more gets built.

Thursday

Her answers go in, her domain is switched over, and manelondon.co.uk is live.

From then on

New prices, new offers, new team members: a WhatsApp message, live the same day. No logins to learn, no waiting on a designer.
