What to expect at a Kent AI meet-up

Come share what you're building, ask what you've been wondering, or just sit and listen. There's no agenda, no pitch decks, no slide deck of hot takes — just a proper conversation about what's working with AI right now and what isn't.

Bring a friend if you like. Builders, lurkers, and curious newcomers all welcome. There's no pressure to drink, eat, or speak if you don't want to.

Anyone, any level

People who use AI every day, people who haven't yet, people who are sceptical. All welcome.

No selling

This isn't a workshop, a sales pitch, or a network event. Genuinely just conversation.

Real questions

Bring something you're stuck on. Or something that worked. Or just listen — that's fine too.

Quiet alcove

The Bull reserves an alcove for the group, so it's easy to hear without shouting.

Who runs Kent AI

James Edwards — Tailored Tools. Based in Maidstone, runs a couple of small businesses, and is part of the Claude Community Ambassador programme. Kent AI exists because nobody local was running anything like it, and AI conversations are far too useful to leave entirely to LinkedIn.

If you've a question before the night, drop a line to hello@tailored-tools.com.

Members come from across Kent — Maidstone, Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells, Sevenoaks, Ashford, Canterbury, Medway, and beyond. If you're anywhere in the county and curious about AI, you'll fit in fine.
