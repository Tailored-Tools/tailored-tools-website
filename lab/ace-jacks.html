I started with Amy's week, not the website.

The website is the part players see. The work that wears people down sits behind it.

I noticed

Her week was fine. Her new season wasn't.

Amy's weekly admin is under two hours, in her own words. The real pain came every new season, twelve games per venue: duplicating pages, re-attaching 200+ players to every venue, editing score tables by hand.

So I made

A back office designed around her three jobs: enter results, manage players, start a season. A new season becomes one step instead of a day of rebuilding. It's being built now.
